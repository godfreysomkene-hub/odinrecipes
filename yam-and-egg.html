🍠 Yam & Egg Sauce

Soft, delicious yam served with a rich, spicy and flavorful egg sauce.

⏱️ 35 mins 📊 Easy 🍳 Breakfast / Lunch

Ingredients

For the Yam

  • 1 medium-sized yam
  • Water
  • Salt to taste

For the Egg Sauce

  • 4 eggs
  • 3 fresh tomatoes
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 1 large onion
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 fresh pepper
  • Seasoning cube
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

Preparing the Yam

  1. Peel the yam and cut it into medium-sized pieces.
  2. Wash the yam thoroughly.
  3. Place the yam in a pot and add enough water to cover it.
  4. Add some salt.
  5. Boil for about 15–20 minutes, or until the yam is soft.
  6. Drain the water and set the yam aside.

Preparing the Egg Sauce

  1. Chop the tomatoes, pepper and onions.
  2. Heat the cooking oil in a frying pan.
  3. Add the chopped onions and fry for a few minutes.
  4. Add the tomatoes and pepper.
  5. Cook the mixture until the tomatoes become soft and the excess water reduces.
  6. Add seasoning and salt to taste.
  7. Beat the eggs in a bowl.
  8. Pour the eggs into the sauce.
  9. Stir gently until the eggs are cooked through.

Serving

Serve the hot egg sauce alongside the boiled yam. Enjoy your delicious homemade Nigerian-style meal! 🍠🍳
