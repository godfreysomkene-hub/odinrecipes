🍚 Jollof Rice

A delicious and flavorful West African rice dish. A delicious meal

⏱️ 45 mins 📊 Medium 🍚 Lunch

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of rice
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 1 large onion
  • 1/2 cup cooking oil
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • Salt to taste
  • Seasoning cubes

Instructions

  1. Wash the rice and set it aside.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, peppers and onions.
  3. Heat the oil in a large pot.
  4. Add the blended tomato mixture.
  5. Cook the tomato mixture until the excess water reduces.
  6. Add curry powder, thyme, seasoning and salt.
  7. Add the chicken stock.
  8. Add the washed rice and stir.
  9. Cover the pot and cook on low heat.
  10. Cook until the rice is soft and the liquid has been absorbed.
  11. Fluff the rice with a fork.
  12. Serve hot and enjoy!
