🍗 Fried Chicken

Crispy, juicy and perfectly seasoned fried chicken.

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 2 cups of flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup of milk
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Garlic powder
  • Onion powder
  • Cooking oil

Instructions

  1. Wash and clean the chicken pieces.
  2. Season the chicken with salt, pepper, garlic powder and onion powder.
  3. Beat the eggs and mix them with the milk.
  4. Dip each piece of chicken into the egg mixture.
  5. Coat the chicken thoroughly with flour.
  6. Heat the cooking oil in a deep frying pan.
  7. Carefully place the chicken into the hot oil.
  8. Fry until golden brown and fully cooked.
  9. Remove the chicken and place it on a paper towel.
  10. Serve hot and enjoy!
