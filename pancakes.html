🥞 Pancakes

Soft, fluffy and delicious pancakes perfect for breakfast.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of flour
  • 2 tablespoons of sugar
  • 2 teaspoons of baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon of salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups of milk
  • 2 tablespoons of melted butter
  • Vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt in a bowl.
  2. In another bowl, beat the eggs and add the milk.
  3. Add the melted butter and vanilla extract.
  4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients.
  5. Mix gently until everything is combined.
  6. Heat a lightly greased frying pan.
  7. Pour some pancake batter into the pan.
  8. Cook until bubbles appear on the surface.
  9. Flip the pancake and cook the other side until golden brown.
  10. Repeat until all the batter is finished.
  11. Serve with your favorite toppings and enjoy!
